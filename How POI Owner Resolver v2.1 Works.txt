How POI Owner Resolver v2.1 Works

- Identifies your alliance: Uses the alliance of the player currently logged into Tiberium Alliances.
- Retrieves your alliance's POI loss history: Reads the game's POI loss notifications to identify coordinates of POIs your alliance previously lost.
- Identifies the Top 10 alliances: Retrieves the current Top 10 alliances from the game's rankings.
- Retrieves current POI ownership: Queries each of those alliances to obtain its currently owned POIs, including coordinates, type, and level.
- Matches lost POIs to current owners: Determines whether any of your alliance's previously lost POIs are currently owned by one of the Top 10 alliances.
- Provides three views:
- Our Lost POIs: Historical losses and their current owners, when identifiable.
- Current Top 10 POIs: All POIs currently reported as owned by the Top 10 alliances.
- Observed Changes: Ownership differences detected between saved checks.
- Establishes an ownership baseline: On the first run, saves a snapshot of current POI ownership without treating existing POIs as ownership changes.
- Tracks future changes: Compares each subsequent snapshot against the previous one, recording changes in ownership, newly observed POIs, and POIs no longer listed.
- Provides coordinate search: Allows you to locate a specific POI by its X:Y coordinates.
- Filters by alliance: Allows you to narrow the results to a particular alliance.
- Refreshes on demand: Retrieves updated game information when the script loads or you click Refresh Data.
- Preserves tracking information: Saves snapshots and observed changes in your browser's local storage, allowing tracking to continue across sessions.
- Provides an accessible interface: Uses a draggable red button with yellow lettering to open the results window.

Important limitations
- Current owner is not necessarily the alliance that captured the POI from you.
- Only the current Top 10 alliances are checked for ownership.
- Changes occurring between snapshots may be missed.
- Historical loss information depends on notifications still available from the game.
- The script does not continuously monitor ownership while you're playing.

In summary: The resolver identifies POIs your alliance previously lost, shows who owns them today when the owner is among the Top 10, and builds a record of observed ownership changes going forward.
